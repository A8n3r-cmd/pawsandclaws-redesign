Adoption

Find your new best friend

Adopting a new family member is a great way to give our dogs and cats a second chance at a loving, fur-ever home.

View available animals Call 07 4098 5721

Why adopt from us?

Healthy, happy animals ready for homes

Fully vetted and socialised

  • Every dog undergoes a thorough behaviour assessment before being accepted.
  • All animals are vet checked, microchipped and desexed (or desexing is included in the adoption fee for a later date).
  • They are wormed and up to date with vaccinations.
  • We socialise dogs with others of the same energy level.

A careful matching process

We take pride in carefully matching our pets with the right families. On average, 1 in 5 shelter dogs end up back in the shelter, so our structured process is designed to find the best fit and give every adoption the best chance of success.

We ask potential adopters to complete a pre-adoption questionnaire so we can understand your lifestyle, living situation and what you are looking for in a pet. Based on your responses, we recommend suitable animals and schedule a dedicated appointment for you to meet them.
