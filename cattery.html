Cattery

A calm, comfortable retreat for our feline friends and your cat while you're away.

A great space for cats to roam and play

Our brand new cattery at Paws and Claws is a great space for our adoption cats to roam and play. The cattery comprises a large air conditioned indoor area complete with toys, climbing frames and cosy beds for our furry felines to enjoy.

There is also an outdoor area ideal for catching some rays. If you are a cat lover, you are also welcome to come hang with our furry friends.

You can find us at 6-8 Teamsters Close, Craiglie. To meet our cats, pop into the shelter between 10am and 1:30pm.

Donations for the cattery

All of our cat's food and toys have been kindly donated. You are welcome to donate cat food, toys and anything else you think we might need. Drop off at the shelter or find donation boxes at Coles Port Douglas and the IGA.
