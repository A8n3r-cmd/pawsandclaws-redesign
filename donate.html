Current needs

We are always grateful for donations

Currently, we are happily accepting donations of:

  • Cat litter and trays
  • Dog and cat toys
  • Dog and cat beds

If you are unsure whether an item is suitable, please contact us on 07 4098 5721 or email reception@pawsandclaws.org.au.

Please note

We are currently at capacity for any linen (towels, blankets, sheets) and dog food, as we make our own raw food for all of our dogs. Thank you for your understanding.
