Volunteer with us

Our volunteers help us keep the shelter running so we can continue to rescue and rehome animals in need.

Make a difference

Here at Paws and Claws, our volunteers help us to keep our organisation running so that we can continue to rescue and re-home animals. Whether you are on vacation, visiting, or simply looking for some quality time with a furry friend, the Paws and Claws shelter dogs are always eager for an adventure.

Taking them out gives these dogs a chance to experience life outside the shelter and helps us learn more about their personalities, which is essential for finding their forever homes. Just stop by the shelter anytime between 9 AM and 3 PM and we'll pair you with one of our wonderful shelter dogs. We provide water, sunscreen, and poop bags for your convenience.

Volunteers agree to adhere to Paws and Claws policies and procedures and follow the instruction from management and staff at all times. The minimum age to commence volunteering unsupervised is 18 years.

Volunteer opportunities

  • Dog walking
  • Fundraising
  • Charity shop
  • Gardening and maintenance
  • Shelter assistant (cats)
  • Shelter assistant (dogs)

To volunteer you must be a member of the organisation. If accepted, you agree to become a member.
